# Support rota pilot: what we learned

Notes for the leadership meeting on Thursday. Presenter: Rosa Lindqvist, Head of Customer Support. Audience is the leadership team, about 10 minutes, then a decision.

## Why we ran it
- Weekend tickets waited until Monday. Monday mornings were a wall: 180+ tickets on average.
- Two people quit in the summer and both mentioned Monday burnout in exit interviews.
- Idea: split the team into two rotas so someone covers every day, no extra headcount. Ran it for 8 weeks, 7 September to 1 November.

## Results (8-week pilot vs the 8 weeks before)
- Median first response time: 14.2 hours before, 3.1 hours during the pilot.
- Monday backlog at 9am: 184 tickets before, 41 during.
- Customer satisfaction (CSAT): 82% before, 89% during.
- Cost: no new hires. Weekend shifts paid at the existing weekend rate, about 2,300 euros extra per month.

Weekly median first response time in hours, for a chart:
weeks 1-8 before: 13.8, 14.5, 15.1, 13.2, 14.9, 14.0, 13.6, 14.4
weeks 1-8 pilot: 6.2, 4.1, 3.4, 3.0, 2.8, 2.9, 2.7, 3.1

## What customers and the team said
- Customer quote from a survey: "I wrote on Saturday about a broken teapot and had a reply before lunch. That never used to happen." (Mrs P., a regular customer)
- Team survey: 7 of 9 people want to keep the rota. The two who don't want fixed weekends, not rotating ones.

## Old way vs pilot
Old way: Monday to Friday only, everyone on the same hours, weekend tickets wait, Monday backlog.
Pilot: two rotas, 7 days covered, each person works one weekend day in three, a day off in the week instead.

## What it would take to make it permanent
1. December: fix the rota pattern, let people choose fixed or rotating weekends.
2. January: update contracts for the weekend rate (HR, about 4 weeks).
3. February: add weekend cover for the live chat, not just email.
4. March: review the numbers again after a full quarter.

## The ask
- Approve the rota as permanent from 1 December.
- Approve the budget: about 27,600 euros a year in weekend pay.
- Next step if approved: Rosa sends the rota plan to the team on Monday.
