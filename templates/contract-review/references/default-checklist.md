# Default review checklist

Used when the request gives no checklist of its own. These are common commercial positions, not any organisation's playbook; the review says it used them. Each line is written for the customer (the party buying the service). When the reviewer is the supplier, read each line from the other side: for example a liability cap of at least 12 months' fees becomes a cap of no more than 12 months' fees, and the supplier wants the termination rights the customer here gives away.

| # | Topic | Standard position | RED when |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Limitation of liability | Mutual cap of at least 12 months' fees; mutual exclusion of indirect loss; the usual carve-outs (death, personal injury, fraud, confidentiality, data protection) apply to both sides | One side's liability is unlimited or capped far lower than the other's; a cap below 3 months' fees |
| 2 | Indemnities | Mutual and limited to defined claims (IP infringement by the supplier, data breaches caused by a party) | A one-way indemnity for "any" loss or for use of the service, uncapped |
| 3 | Term and renewal | Initial term up to 12 months; renewals no longer than 12 months; notice to stop a renewal no more than 60 days | Automatic renewals longer than 12 months, or a notice window over 90 days |
| 4 | Termination | Both sides can end for material breach with a 30-day cure period; the customer can end for convenience on 90 days' notice | Only one side can terminate for convenience, or fees for the rest of the term fall due on termination |
| 5 | Fees and price changes | Fixed fees; annual increases capped at 5% or inflation; payment within 30 days; late interest at a statutory or modest rate | Uncapped increases, or increases above 10% a year |
| 6 | Data protection | Processing on instructions; breach notice within 48 to 72 hours; sub-processors listed with notice of changes; data returned free and deleted at the end | Personal data processed with no breach timeframe and no sub-processor notice, or the supplier may use the data for its own purposes |
| 7 | Intellectual property | Each side keeps its existing IP; the customer owns its data and anything made specifically for it, or holds a perpetual licence | The supplier owns deliverables made for the customer and the customer's licence ends with the contract |
| 8 | Confidentiality | Mutual, with standard exceptions, lasting at least 3 years after the end | One-way, or shorter than 2 years for sensitive information |
| 9 | Warranties and service levels | Services with reasonable skill and care; an availability target with service credits | No remedy at all when the service misses its target (YELLOW, not RED, on its own) |
| 10 | Assignment | Neither side assigns without consent, except to a group company or a buyer of the business | Only one side needs consent |
| 11 | Governing law | The law and courts of the customer's home country | A foreign or unusual jurisdiction with mandatory arbitration |

Also note anything material that is missing (for example no data processing terms when personal data is shared, or no exit assistance), and anything unusual that the checklist does not cover.
