# Why do I see two charges for my subscription?

> Sample help article, invented for a Pulsar template. Tallyhop is a fictional product.

Tallyhop bills each workspace once per billing period, on the date you first subscribed. If your bank shows two charges, it is usually one of the cases below.

## A pending authorisation

When you add or update a card, we place a temporary authorisation of GBP 1.00 (or the same amount in your currency) to check the card. It shows as pending and drops off on its own within 3 to 5 business days. It is never collected.

## A plan change in the same month

Moving to a bigger plan part-way through a billing period creates a separate, prorated charge for the rest of that period. Both charges appear on your invoice list under Settings > Billing > Invoices, each with its own invoice.

## A real duplicate charge

If two full charges for the same period have both completed (not pending) and you did not change your plan, it is a duplicate. Our billing team refunds the duplicate charge in full to the original card. Refunds reach the card within 5 to 7 business days of being issued, depending on your bank.

To speed this up, the agent handling it needs:

- the email address of the workspace owner;
- the date and amount of both charges;
- the last four digits of the card.

Only the billing team can issue a refund. Support agents should route duplicate charges to Billing and confirm to the customer that a refund will be reviewed, not promise one before Billing has checked.
