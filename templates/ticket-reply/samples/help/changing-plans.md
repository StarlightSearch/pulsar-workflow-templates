# Changing your plan

> Sample help article, invented for a Pulsar template. Tallyhop is a fictional product.

Tallyhop has three plans: Solo (GBP 12 a month for one person), Team (GBP 48 a month for up to 10 people) and Business (GBP 9 per person per month, billed for at least 10 people).

## Upgrading

Upgrades take effect at once. You pay a prorated amount for the rest of the current billing period, charged straight away, and the full new price from the next billing date.

## Downgrading

Downgrades take effect at the end of the current billing period. Nothing is refunded for the current period. If your team has more people than the new plan allows, remove people before the change, or it will not go through.

## Who can change the plan

Only the workspace owner can change the plan, under Settings > Billing > Plan.
