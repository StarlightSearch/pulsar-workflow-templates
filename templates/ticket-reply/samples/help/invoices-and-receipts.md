# Downloading invoices and receipts

> Sample help article, invented for a Pulsar template. Tallyhop is a fictional product.

Every payment in Tallyhop has a PDF invoice that doubles as a receipt.

## Download an invoice

1. Go to Settings > Billing > Invoices.
2. Find the payment by date and click Download PDF.

Only workspace owners and billing admins can see the Invoices page.

## Add your company details

Invoices show the details saved under Settings > Billing > Billing details: company name, address and VAT number. To add or change them, edit that page and click Save. Every invoice you download afterwards shows the new details, including invoices for past payments.

We cannot edit an invoice by hand or send a separate VAT invoice. Updating your billing details and downloading the invoice again is the way to get a VAT-compliant invoice.
